## Hybrid delivery decision — authoritative

The final icon package is **hybrid SVG/PNG**, not SVG-only.

Rules:

- use simplified SVG where additional raster detail has no visible benefit;
- use PNG where material, lighting, texture, or painted detail is visibly superior;
- one canonical may use SVG at smaller sizes and optimized PNG variants at larger sizes;
- raster-capable detailed artwork starts from a high-resolution master, initially around 1024×1024 or larger if the visual test justifies it;
- each emitted PNG size must be reviewed and may require size-specific optimization rather than blind downscaling;
- the SVG→PNG crossover is **not frozen yet**;
- determine that crossover visually through A/B comparison at representative sizes;
- do not use embedded-PNG-in-SVG as the default delivery mechanism;
- `style_class` controls visual/review language, not file format.

No delivery manifest exists yet by design. Add structured delivery metadata only if the crossover pilot proves that class/family rules are insufficient and per-canonical exceptions are required.

The current `tools/build-icons.py` scalable-only staging behavior remains a development scaffold and must be upgraded before final v1 packaging.

## MIME/Filetypes art direction

MIME/Filetypes use the **Emblem** class.

Primary family rule:

- keep strong shared file/document family construction;
- distinguish types through one strong symbol, seal, material, color placement, or structural cue;
- do not rely on tiny literal text;
- any letter-like mark must be vector geometry, not font-dependent text;
- preserve recognition at compact file-manager sizes;
- use Witcher materials and restrained accents without turning every filetype into an unrelated miniature scene.

Mandatory Emblem review sizes remain:

- 22 px
- 32 px
- 48 px
- 64 px
- 128 px

Review on:

- `#0A151E`
- `#171A1C`
- `#1C1813`
- `#262729`
- generic light edge case `#F2F0EA`

These are review sizes, not a frozen PNG/SVG crossover ladder.
