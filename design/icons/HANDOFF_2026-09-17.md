## Hybrid delivery decision — authoritative

The final icon package is **hybrid SVG/PNG**, not SVG-only.

Rules:

- use simplified SVG where additional raster detail has no visible benefit;
- use PNG where material, lighting, texture, or painted detail is visibly superior;
- one canonical may use SVG at smaller sizes and optimized PNG variants at larger sizes;
- raster-capable detailed artwork starts from a high-resolution master, initially around 1024×1024 or larger if visual testing justifies it;
- each emitted PNG size must be reviewed and may require size-specific optimization rather than blind downscaling;
- the SVG→PNG crossover is deliberately **not frozen yet**;
- determine that crossover visually through A/B comparison at representative sizes;
- do not use embedded-PNG-in-SVG as the default delivery mechanism;
- `style_class` controls visual/review language, not file format.

No delivery manifest exists yet by design. Add structured delivery metadata only if the crossover pilot shows that class/family rules are insufficient and per-canonical exceptions are required.

The current `tools/build-icons.py` scalable-only staging behavior remains a development scaffold and must be upgraded before final v1 packaging.

## Categories/Misc art direction

Categories/Misc uses the **Emblem** class.

Keep:

- one strong category/tool metaphor per icon;
- coherent forged-metal, leather, parchment, crystal, gear, or control language;
- readable silhouettes at compact desktop sizes;
- restrained Witcher red;
- no copied vendor or Witcher game assets;
- no font-dependent text;
- no unnecessary micro-detail.

Mandatory Emblem review sizes:

- 22 px
- 32 px
- 48 px
- 64 px
- 128 px

Review surfaces:

- `#0A151E`
- `#171A1C`
- `#1C1813`
- `#262729`
- light edge case `#F2F0EA`

These review sizes do not define the future PNG/SVG crossover.
